# Business templates

Picked once at sign-up, a template just sets starting defaults — categories, units, sample
products, and which modules are on by default. Everything it sets can be changed afterwards from
Settings; nothing is locked in.

| Template | Tagline | Starts with |
|---|---|---|
| **Food store / frozen foods** | Quality frozen foods, fresh to your door. | Weight-based stock (Kg, cartons), size-graded SKUs, cash services, referrals, pickup orders, online store. This is what Zendoria itself runs on. |
| **General retail / supermarket** | Everything you need, one stop. | Item-count and pack-based stock, the [retail pack](../modules/retail-pack.md), referrals, pickup orders, online store, cameras. |
| **Pharmacy** | Your health, in stock. | Item-count stock, the [pharmacy pack](../modules/pharmacy.md), pickup orders, online store, cameras. No cash services or referrals by default. |
| **Boutique / fashion** | Every size, every style, in stock. | Retail pack, referrals, pickup orders, online store, cameras. *No first-class size/colour variants yet — see [Products & inventory](../modules/products-and-inventory.md).* |
| **Cosmetics & beauty** | Every shade, every size, in stock. | Same as boutique. *Same variant limitation applies.* |
| **Electronics** | Every device, tracked and in stock. | Retail pack (barcode/IMEI field), referrals, pickup orders, online store, cameras. *No true serial-number sales history yet.* |
| **Hardware store** | Every nut, bolt and bag, accounted for. | Retail pack, multiple units (Piece/Kg/Length/Carton), per-sale discounts, referrals, pickup orders, online store, cameras. |
| **Restaurant / eatery** | From the kitchen to the till. | General POS for finished menu items, referrals, pickup orders, online store, cameras. *No recipe/ingredient deduction yet.* |
| **Wholesale distributor** | Bulk stock, tracked to the last carton. | Retail pack, cash services, dispatcher tracking, carton pricing. No referrals/pickup/online store/cameras by default — mostly B2B. |
| **Provision store** | Every sale, every stock count, in one place. | The simplest starting point: free product codes, low-stock alerts, the same POS everyone gets. Nothing extra on by default — turn on anything later once the basics are working. |

Not sure which fits? Pick the closest match — a boutique selling electronics accessories, for
example, can start on either template and turn on/off individual modules afterwards regardless.
