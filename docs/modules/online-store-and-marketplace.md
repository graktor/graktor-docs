# Online store & marketplace

*Plan/template-gated — feature key `onlineStore`.*

- **Your own online store** — every business with this switched on gets its own public storefront,
  automatically kept in sync with in-store stock and pricing. Nothing needs to be re-typed on a
  separate platform.
- **Shared marketplace** — a single marketplace site lists products **across every business**
  that has an online store switched on, so a shopper can search and find your products without
  needing to know your store exists first.
- Orders placed online can be fulfilled as [pickup orders](./pickup-orders.md) or delivery, and
  paid for with a customer's [wallet](./customers-and-crm.md) or online payment (if the
  [online payments add-on](../billing-and-plans.md#add-ons) is on).
