# Sale deposits & balance due

*Service-business tool.*

A deposit against one specific sale -- a tailoring job that takes 40% now and the rest on
collection, a watch repair that needs a deposit at intake. Distinct from a customer's general
credit balance ([Finance](./finance.md)): the amount still owed is tracked against that one sale,
not mixed into a running tab.

- At checkout, a cashier can record less than the full amount as paid now; the shortfall becomes
  that sale's own balance due.
- Further payments can be recorded against the sale over time, each one logged, until it's fully
  paid.
- Paying more than what's left is simply capped at what's owed -- never overcharges the record.

## Honesty note

No due-date/overdue-reminder automation yet -- staff record payments by hand as they come in.
