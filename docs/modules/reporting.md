# Reporting & dashboard

- Sales and slow-mover reports, available to every business regardless of template or plan.
- **Owner dashboard** — capital invested vs. cumulative net profit per branch, remaining distance
  to breakeven, and a projected breakeven date from a 3-month rolling average.
- Admin/Manager access; a narrower view is visible to Admin only for business-wide figures.
