# School pack

*Industry module — feature key `school`.*

Turns Graktor into a school management system alongside (or instead of) retail: students, classes,
fees, attendance, report cards, computer-based testing, and parent/student self-service, all under
the same login as the rest of the business.

- **Students & classes** — admission number, date of birth, guardian contact, class/arm assignment.
- **Fees** — a running owed balance per student, charges and payments, with online payment support.
- **Attendance** — a daily per-class roll-call (Present/Absent/Late/Excused), with a same-day tally
  and an all-time summary per student.
- **Report cards** — subject scores and grades per term, with an AI-drafted remark you can edit
  before saving.
- **Computer-based testing (CBT)** — a reusable question bank, scheduled exams, auto-graded
  multiple-choice/true-false, and manual grading for short-answer/essay.
- **AI Studio** — generate lesson plans, notes, schemes of work and worksheets.
- **Parent & student portals** — a separate, PIN-based login for guardians and students to check
  fees, attendance and results, with no access to staff-side data.
- **Birthday alerts** — a student with a birthday in the next 7 days shows up as a badge on the
  Students page and a sidebar nag, so it's never missed.

## Staff access

School has its own area in [custom staff roles](./users-and-roles.md): a business that turns
School on gets a ready-made **Teacher** role (scoped to just Students/Classes/Attendance/Report
Cards/Exams/AI Studio — never billing, staff, or other inventory), which the owner can assign
directly or adjust.
