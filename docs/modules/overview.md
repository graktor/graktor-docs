# Modules overview

Graktor is made of modules. Every business gets the core ones (sales, products, customers, users,
finance); the rest are free [add-on](../billing-and-plans.md#add-ons) toggles, on or off per
business regardless of plan — your [template](../business-templates/overview.md) just picks
sensible defaults when you sign up. Plans differentiate on branch/staff/product limits and the
number of industry modules (Pharmacy, Retail, School, Cameras) included, not on which features are
switched on.

## Core (every business)

- [Point of sale](./point-of-sale.md) — ringing up sales, held sales, returns, offline mode.
- [Products & inventory](./products-and-inventory.md) — stock, pricing, low-stock alerts, batch
  tracing.
- [Customers & CRM](./customers-and-crm.md) — customer records, wallets, referral rewards,
  self-purchase cashback.
- [Branches](./branches.md) — multiple locations under one login.
- [Users & roles](./users-and-roles.md) — staff accounts and permissions.
- [Finance](./finance.md) — expenses, capital investments, cash reconciliation.
- [Reporting & dashboard](./reporting.md) — sales, slow-movers, and business health at a glance.

## Add-on toggles

- [Pickup orders](./pickup-orders.md)
- [Online store & marketplace](./online-store-and-marketplace.md)
- [Cash services (POS agent)](./cash-services.md)
- [Cameras & the Camera Bridge](./cameras.md)
- [Pharmacy pack](./pharmacy.md)
- [Retail pack](./retail-pack.md)

  **Billing → Add-ons**.

## Service-business tools

Built for the [49 service-business templates](../business-templates/service-businesses.md), but
available to any business:

- [Tickets](./tickets.md) — a job/status workflow for repair, tailoring, laundry and similar work.
- [Custom fields](./custom-fields.md) — a business's own free-form fields (IMEI, plate number,
  measurements, case number...).
- [Bill of materials](./bill-of-materials.md) — a service that consumes other stocked components.
- [Sale deposits & balance due](./sale-deposits.md) — take a deposit now, collect the rest later.
- [Staff assignment & commission](./staff-and-commission.md) — attribute a sale line to a staff
  member with a commission percentage.
- [Calendar & booking](./calendar-and-booking.md) — appointment capacity for a staff member or a
  named resource (a room, a bay).
- [Serialized assets](./serialized-assets.md) — individually-tracked units (a specific vehicle, a
  specific panel) with their own warranty date.
- [Session packs](./session-packs.md) — prepaid units a customer buys and redeems over time.
- [Photos & document attachments](./attachments.md) — attach a photo or document to a customer or
  ticket.

## Honesty note

Every page here says plainly what's actually built versus what's planned. If a page doesn't
mention something you need, it almost certainly isn't there yet — ask rather than assume.
