# Modules overview

Graktor is made of modules. Every business gets the core ones (sales, products, customers, users,
finance); the rest switch on based on your [template](../business-templates/overview.md), your
[plan](../billing-and-plans.md), or a one-off [add-on](../billing-and-plans.md#add-ons) toggle.

## Core (every business)

- [Point of sale](./point-of-sale.md) — ringing up sales, held sales, returns, offline mode.
- [Products & inventory](./products-and-inventory.md) — stock, pricing, low-stock alerts, batch
  tracing.
- [Customers & CRM](./customers-and-crm.md) — customer records, wallets, referral rewards,
  self-purchase cashback.
- [Branches](./branches.md) — multiple locations under one login.
- [Users & roles](./users-and-roles.md) — staff accounts and permissions.
- [Finance](./finance.md) — expenses, capital investments, cash reconciliation.
- [Reporting & dashboard](./reporting.md) — sales, slow-movers, and business health at a glance.
