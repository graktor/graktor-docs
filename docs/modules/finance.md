# Finance

- **Expenses** — record business expenses (Admin/Manager).
- **Capital investments** — track money put into the business, feeding the owner dashboard's
  breakeven projection (see [Reporting](./reporting.md)).
- **Vendors** — supplier records.
- **Cash & transfer reconciliation** — what the system expects was collected in cash and by
  transfer for a branch/period (from completed sales), against what was actually counted/seen in
  the bank, so a shortfall or an unbanked amount surfaces on its own rather than needing a manual
  spreadsheet cross-check.
- **Change owed** — if a cashier can't give exact change, it's recorded against the customer and
  automatically deducted from their next sale. Admin/Manager can see and correct every customer's
  outstanding change balance.
- **Credit owed** — the inverse: what customers owe the business, with repayment tracking (see
  [Customers & CRM](./customers-and-crm.md)).
- **Invoices & waybills** — formal, sequentially-numbered documents per tenant; a waybill can
  optionally tie to a sale and name a dispatcher.
