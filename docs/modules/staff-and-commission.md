# Staff assignment & commission

*Service-business tool.*

Attribute one line of a sale to the staff member who actually performed it -- a barber, a nail
technician, a mechanic -- separate from whoever rang up the till. A commission percentage on that
line is calculated and recorded at the moment of sale, so it stays accurate even if prices or
rates change later.

- Every commission earned is kept as its own record, reportable per staff member.
- Payout itself is recorded the same way any other staff payment is -- through
  [Finance](./finance.md)'s salary/expense tracking.

## Honesty note

One staff member per sale line -- no splitting a single line's commission between two people.
