# Users & roles

Three roles, in increasing order of access:

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| **Cashier** | Run the till, hold/resume sales, look up customers, process wallet-funded sales, clock in/out, record pharmacy dispensing. |
| **Manager** | Everything a Cashier can, plus: manage products, expenses, capital investments, vendors, dispatchers, customers' credit/change balances, reconciliation, returns, gift card issuing, messaging, reporting/dashboard. |
| **Admin** | Everything, plus: staff accounts and roles, branches, billing/plan, cameras, Camera Bridge pairing, business settings. |

## Attendance

Clock-in/clock-out shift tracking for Cashiers and Managers. Everyone can see their own hours;
Admin and Manager can additionally see any staff member's hours — no one else can see anyone
else's.
