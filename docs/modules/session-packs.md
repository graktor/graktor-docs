# Session packs

*Service-business tool.*

Prepaid units a customer buys up front and redeems over time -- a 10-session gym pack, a 5-massage
pack, a term of tutoring lessons. Define a pack once (how many units, how many days until they
expire, if any); link it to a product and selling that product grants the pack automatically, or
grant/redeem it by hand at any time.

- A customer can hold several packs of the same kind at once (buying another 10-pack after using
  up the first); redeeming always draws from the oldest one first.
- Redeeming more units than are left is refused, telling the customer exactly how many remain.

## Honesty note

No auto-renewal -- a pack simply runs out; selling another is how a customer tops up.
