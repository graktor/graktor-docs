# Customers & CRM

## What it does

- Customer records: name, phone, purchase history.
- **Wallet** — a customer can carry a balance, funded at the shop or via a redeemed gift card, and
  spend it at the till or on the online store. The balance only ever changes through the same
  guarded ledger update as every other wallet operation, with a matching transaction row for every
  change — no silent balance edits.
- **Credit** — Admin/Manager can set how much a customer is allowed to owe at once, let a sale put
  part of its total on credit, see every customer's outstanding balance, and record repayments.
- **Referral rewards** *(plan/add-on-gated)* — when a referred customer makes a purchase, the
  referrer earns a percentage of that sale's net profit, credited to the referrer's own wallet.
- **Self-purchase cashback** — a business can turn on a percentage (its own choice) of a
  customer's own purchase's gross profit, credited straight back to that same customer's wallet.
  This runs independently of referral rewards — both can fire on the same sale.

## Roles

Admin/Manager handle credit limits and repayments; any signed-in role can look up a customer and
process a wallet-funded sale.
