# Pickup orders

*Plan/template-gated — feature key `pickupOrders`.*

A customer orders online or by phone, and picks the order up at a branch rather than having it
delivered. Staff see pending pickup orders and mark them ready/collected; the customer can be
notified automatically when it's ready (SMS, if the [SMS notifications add-on](../billing-and-plans.md#add-ons)
is on).
