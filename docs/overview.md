# What is Graktor?

Graktor (built by Zendoria Ventures) is a point-of-sale, inventory, CRM and finance platform for
Nigerian retail businesses — one shop or many branches, on one login.

It runs as:

- A **web admin app**, usable from any browser.
- An **installable Windows app**.
- An **Android app**.
- A **public online store** for each business, and a shared **marketplace** that lists products
  across businesses.

Every business picks a **template** when it signs up — food store, pharmacy, boutique, hardware,
electronics, and more — which sets sensible defaults for categories, units, and which modules are
switched on. Nothing about the template is permanent: any module can be turned on or off later
from Settings.

## Who this is for

- A single shop moving off paper records or a notebook.
- A multi-branch business that needs one login across all its branches, with stock and sales
  visible centrally.
- A business that wants an online store that stays in sync with in-store stock automatically,
  without re-typing anything.
